About

Hi, I'm Diana. I'm a product designer working on experiences that aim to be truly useful.

I'm drawn to design that doesn't interrupt: the kind that helps something complex feel clear and usable.

Curiosity is always my compass. A decade across brand, web and product means I catch things a single-track designer wouldn't. Always down to talk design, tech, or the weird overlap between them.

Before product, I spent years in brand and agency work: identities, websites and communication for hospitality and real estate. That is where I learned to care about how something feels the moment you arrive.

I love to travel, and museums are the first place I visit.
